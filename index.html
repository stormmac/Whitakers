Since 1903

Four generations who know old jewellery

Whitakers has been making jewellery in the Hunter since 1903. Today John Whitaker, a GIA-trained gemmologist, and his daughter Eden see the same family pieces come back through the door, this time to be made new.

We'll tell you honestly what can be reused, what needs care, and what will look its best in a modern setting.

Good to know

Quick answers

Anything else? Just ask Dee.

Do I need to come in?

No. You can start with photos and do most of it from your phone. You're always welcome in the Charlestown showroom if you'd rather see us in person.

Will I get my own stones back?

Yes. Your stones are recorded when we receive them and set into your new design.

Can you use my old gold?

Usually, yes. Old gold can be refined and recast, or credited towards the new piece.

Can you combine pieces from different family members?

Yes. Stones from several pieces can come together in one design, or one heirloom can be split between siblings.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the design and how much of your material we reuse. You'll get a clear quote with your design, before any work starts.
