The Whitakers experience

From first sketch to forever, in four steps

You see and approve your piece before any metal is cast. Change anything along the way.

01

Consult

Sit down with John or the team in our showroom. Bring inspiration, a budget, or nothing at all.

02

Design

We sketch and build your piece in CAD, choosing the diamond or gemstone together.

03

Preview

See a photo-real 3D render, then try on a resin model so you know exactly how it will sit.

04

Craft

Your piece is cast, set and finished by hand, ready to wear for a lifetime.

Since 1903

Four generations at the bench

Reginald Wilfred Whitaker opened the first Whitakers in Muswellbrook in 1903 with his three sons. More than a century later, John Whitaker and his daughter Eden carry on the family craft from our Charlestown showroom.

Every piece is personal to John and the team. We encourage you to chop and change until it's unmistakably yours.
