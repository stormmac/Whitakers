Good to know

Heirloom questions

Ask us anything else at your consultation, or tap Dee in the corner.

Can you use my old gold for the new piece?

Usually, yes. Old gold can be refined and recast, or credited towards the new piece. We'll check the carat and condition first.

Will I get my own stones back?

Yes. Your stones are recorded at the consultation and set into your new design.

What if a stone is chipped or worn?

Older stones often have small chips or wear. We'll show you under magnification and talk about recutting, protective settings or replacing that stone.

Can you combine pieces from different family members?

Absolutely. Stones from several pieces can come together in one design, or one heirloom can be split into pieces for each sibling.

How much does a redesign cost?

It depends on the design and how much of your material we reuse. You'll get a clear quote once the design is agreed, before any work starts.
